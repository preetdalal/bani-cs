# Bani's CS Executive Study Tracker (ICSI Syllabus 2022)

A dedicated, personalized modern study companion website created exclusively for Bani's Company Secretary (CS) Executive Programme preparation.

## Key Features

1. **Password Protected Workspace:**
   - Password: `tajkichai`
   - Unlock state is remembered on the device for seamless studying, with a manual Lock button in the top navigation.

2. **Complete ICSI 2022 Syllabus Coverage:**
   - **Group 1 (400 Marks):**
     - Paper 1: Jurisprudence, Interpretation & General Laws (JIGL)
     - Paper 2: Company Law & Practice (CLP)
     - Paper 3: Setting Up of Business, Industrial & Labour Laws (SBILL)
     - Paper 4: Corporate Accounting & Financial Management (CAFM)
   - **Group 2 (300 Marks):**
     - Paper 5: Capital Market & Securities Laws (CMSL)
     - Paper 6: Economic, Commercial & Intellectual Property Laws (ECIPL)
     - Paper 7: Tax Laws and Practice (TLP)

3. **Persistent Device Memory (Local Storage):**
   - Checkboxes next to every chapter save automatically to device browser memory.
   - 3-stage Revision Tracker (R1, R2, R3) for multiple rounds of revision.
   - Star/Bookmark system to highlight challenging or high-yield chapters.
   - Personal chapter-wise notes drawer with automatic local saving.

4. **Rich Blue Aesthetic & Dashboard:**
   - Premium sapphire and royal navy glassmorphic design.
   - Animated SVG circular gauge for overall preparation percentage.
   - Real-time Group 1 and Group 2 progress bars.
   - Live chapter completion counters and revision tallies.

5. **Integrated Study Productivity Tools:**
   - **Pomodoro Focus Timer:** 25m / 50m study intervals with break modes and built-in synthesizer chimes.
   - **Exam Target Countdown:** Live countdown for upcoming exam sessions.
   - **Real-time Search & Filter:** Instant search by chapter name, concept keywords, or filter by status.
   - **Data Backup & Restore:** Export and import progress via JSON.
   - **Printable Checklist:** One-click print stylesheet for offline tracking.

## Getting Started

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
When prompted, enter the password: `tajkichai`
